Fix Wix Favicon Not Showing in Google Search Results – Quick Guide

If you’ve just published a Wix site and notice the brand icon missing from Google’s search listings, the issue is usually a tiny mismatch between what Google expects and what Wix actually serves.

Quick fix – force Google to see a valid favicon

The fastest way to get the icon back in SERPs is to add a proper rel='icon' link on the homepage and then request a recrawl in Google Search Console.

  1. Upload a 48 × 48 px PNG (or an SVG) via the Wix editor – see Wix’s guide here.
  2. Open the Wix Dashboard, go to Settings → Advanced → Custom Code, and paste the following tag into the <head> section:
&lt;link rel='icon' href='https://example.com/favicon.png' sizes='48x48' type='image/png'&gt;

Save and republish the site. Then open Google Search Console, enter your home‑page URL in the URL Inspection tool, and click “Request Indexing”. Google will fetch the new icon on its next crawl.

Why the quick fix works – Google’s favicon requirements

Google only displays a favicon in search results when it can retrieve a square image of at least 48 × 48 px that is reachable without authentication. The official specification is described in Google’s Search documentation here.

Common root causes on Wix

  • Default “W” icon still active. Wix keeps the placeholder until you replace it in the editor, so crawlers keep seeing the generic icon.
  • Missing rel='icon' tag. Wix may rely on an implicit /favicon.ico file, which Google does not prioritize over an explicit link tag.
  • Incorrect image size or format. PNGs smaller than 48 × 48 px, ICO files without the required size, or SVGs lacking proper viewBox can be ignored.
  • Robots.txt or X‑Robots‑Tag blocking the file. If the favicon URL is disallowed, Google cannot crawl it and falls back to no icon.

Each of these issues can be verified with the HTML spec for link icons here and by inspecting the network request in your browser’s DevTools.

Testing the fix locally before Google sees it

After adding the tag, open the page in Chrome, right‑click → Inspect, and look for the link[rel='icon'] element in the Elements panel. Verify that the href resolves to a 200 response and that the image dimensions are correct (you can view the image in the Sources tab).

If the icon appears in the browser tab but not in Google, double‑check that the URL is absolute, uses HTTPS, and is not redirected to a 404 page.

Prevention – keep your Wix favicon visible long term

Once the icon is indexed, adopt these habits to avoid future disappearances:

  1. Store the favicon at the root of your domain (e.g., https://example.com/favicon.png) and keep the file publicly accessible.
  2. Include both a rel='icon' tag and a fallback rel='shortcut icon' for legacy browsers.
  3. Append a cache‑busting query string whenever you replace the image, such as favicon.png?v=2, to force browsers and Google to fetch the newest version.
  4. Run the favicon checker after any change to confirm all required sizes and tags are present.
  5. Submit or update your XML sitemap so Google revisits the homepage after each icon update.

Following this checklist reduces the chance that a future redesign or a CDN configuration will unintentionally hide the icon from crawlers.

Conclusion

Missing Wix favicons in Google search results are almost always a simple configuration gap. Adding a correct rel='icon' tag, serving a 48 × 48 px PNG (or a compliant SVG), and prompting Google to recrawl restores brand visibility within minutes. Keep the preventive checklist handy, and your favicon will stay visible in both browsers and search results.
