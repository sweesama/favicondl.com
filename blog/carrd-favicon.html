Carrd Favicon: Add a Custom Icon Step by Step

If you've ever published a Carrd site and then noticed a generic globe or a blank square in the browser tab, you're not alone. Carrd does not add a favicon by default, so the tab shows whatever the browser falls back to. The fix is simple once you know where the setting lives.

This guide walks you through adding a custom Carrd favicon, verifying it, and handling the most common reasons it might not appear. We'll keep it practical — no prior image-editing experience required.

What you need before you start

Carrd accepts a single image file for the site icon. To get a crisp result, prepare a square PNG with a transparent background, ideally 512×512 pixels or larger. The platform will resize it for the tab, but starting with a high-resolution square avoids blur.

If you only have a logo with text or a wide aspect ratio, crop it to the core mark first. A favicon is read at 16×16 pixels in most tabs, so detail disappears fast. For more on that design constraint, see our guide on why a favicon is not just a resized logo.

Step-by-step: add a Carrd favicon

  1. Open your Carrd dashboard. Log in and select the site you want to edit.
  2. Go to Settings. In the editor, click the gear icon or the Settings tab in the left sidebar.
  3. Find the Favicon section. Scroll to the Favicon area. You'll see an upload button and a preview box.
  4. Upload your square PNG. Click Upload, choose your file, and wait for the preview to update. Carrd stores the image and serves it from its CDN.
  5. Publish your site. Carrd only applies changes to the live site after you click Publish. If you're on a free plan, the favicon may only appear on the published URL, not the editor preview.
  6. Verify in a browser tab. Open your live site in a new tab. The icon should appear within a few seconds. If it doesn't, try a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R).

That's the entire native workflow. Carrd does not expose a field for multiple icon sizes or an Apple Touch Icon, so the single upload is all you get through the UI.

What Carrd actually outputs

When you upload a favicon, Carrd generates a <link rel='icon'> tag in the page head pointing to the uploaded image. The exact markup is handled by the platform, so you don't need to edit HTML. The HTML specification defines rel=icon as the standard way to associate an icon with a page, and browsers follow that convention.

Because Carrd serves the icon from its own domain, you cannot easily add additional sizes like a 180×180 Apple Touch Icon or a web app manifest through the standard editor. If you need those, you'll have to use Carrd's embed feature or a custom domain setup with additional HTML injection (available on some plans).

Common pitfalls and how to fix them

  • The icon looks blurry. You probably uploaded a small or non-square image. Replace it with a 512×512 PNG and republish.
  • The old icon still shows. Browsers cache favicons aggressively. Clear your cache or test in a private window. For a deeper fix, see our favicon not showing checklist.
  • Nothing appears at all. Confirm you clicked Publish after uploading. Carrd does not auto-save the favicon to the live site.
  • Google Search shows a generic icon. Google needs time to recrawl your site. The requirements are documented: a square icon at least 8×8 pixels, a stable URL, and a rel=icon link on the homepage. Carrd's upload satisfies the basics, but recrawling can take days or weeks.

Conclusion

Adding a Carrd favicon takes less than two minutes: upload a square PNG in Settings, publish, and verify in a fresh tab. The main friction is caching and the platform's single-file limitation. Start with a clean, high-contrast mark, and your tab will look intentional instead of generic.
