How this compares with a normal favicon set

A site you publish yourself usually needs a different set from the one Brave happens to ship: 16×16, 32×32 and 48×48 in one favicon.ico, a 180×180 apple-touch icon, and 192×192 plus 512×512 manifest PNGs. FAQ

What is the Brave favicon.ico URL?

Yes. favicon.ico (13262 bytes) from https://brave.com/favicon.ico. Directory sizes: 16×16, 24×24, 48×48.

What are the Brave favicon dimensions?

The pixel sizes below were read from the files, not from a generic size chart. webp-180x180-01.webp: 180×180 (WebP VP8X header) favicon.ico: 16×16, 24×24, 48×48 (ICO directory) webp-192x192-03.webp: 192×192 (WebP VP8X header) png-32x32-04.png: 32×32 (PNG IHDR)

What apple-touch-icon does Brave publish?

Yes. https://brave.com/static-assets/images/cropped-brave_appicon_release-180x180.png saved as webp-180x180-01.webp.

What brand colour does the Brave favicon page record?

#ff4400, from the most common non-white pixel in png-32x32-04.png, quantised to 4 bits per channel.

Which icon files were saved for Brave?

On 12 November 2026 these image files were downloaded for brave.com: webp-180x180-01.webp (webp, 4876 bytes, 180×180); favicon.ico (ico, 13262 bytes, 16×16, 24×24, 48×48); webp-192x192-03.webp (webp, 5296 bytes, 192×192); png-32x32-04.png (png, 1103 bytes, 32×32).

May I use the Brave icon on my own site?

No. Logos and favicons belong to their owners and are shown here for reference so you can see the real format and dimensions. For your own site, make a file with the PNG to favicon converter or download a site you control.
