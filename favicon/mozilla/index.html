How this compares with a normal favicon set

A site you publish yourself usually needs a different set from the one Mozilla happens to ship: 16×16, 32×32 and 48×48 in one favicon.ico, a 180×180 apple-touch icon, and 192×192 plus 512×512 manifest PNGs. That chart, including 96×96, maskable icons, Windows tiles and SVG, is the favicon dimensions guide. Use Mozilla ’s files as a reference, then build your own with the PNG to favicon converter.

FAQ

What is the Mozilla favicon.ico URL?

Yes. favicon.ico (1409 bytes) from https://www.mozilla.org/media/img/favicons/mozilla/favicon.d0be64e474b1.ico. Directory sizes: 16×16, 32×32, 64×64.

What are the Mozilla favicon dimensions?

The pixel sizes below were read from the files, not from a generic size chart. png-180x180-01.png: 180×180 (PNG IHDR) favicon.ico: 16×16, 32×32, 64×64 (ICO directory) ico-64x64-02.png: 64×64 (PNG payload inside the ICO. Not resized.) png-196x196-03.png: 196×196 (PNG IHDR)

What apple-touch-icon does Mozilla publish?

Yes. https://www.mozilla.org/media/img/favicons/mozilla/apple-touch-icon.05aa000f6748.png saved as png-180x180-01.png.

What brand colour does the Mozilla favicon page record?

#221122, from the most common non-white pixel in png-196x196-03.png, quantised to 4 bits per channel.

Which icon files were saved for Mozilla?

On 12 November 2026 these image files were downloaded for mozilla.org: png-180x180-01.png (png, 467 bytes, 180×180); favicon.ico (ico, 1409 bytes, 16×16, 32×32, 64×64); ico-64x64-02.png (png, 315 bytes, 64×64); png-196x196-03.png (png, 591 bytes, 196×196).

May I use the Mozilla icon on my own site?

No. Logos and favicons belong to their owners and are shown here for reference so you can see the real format and dimensions. For your own site, make a file with the PNG to favicon converter or download a site you control.
