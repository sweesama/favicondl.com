Logos and favicons belong to their owners and are shown here for reference. This is not an endorsement and it is not a license to use the mark.

Pinterest’s icon is the “P” mark pinterest.com publishes for boards and pins. The page lists the real URL, format and dimensions of the files that were saved.

Checked 12 November 2026 against pinterest.com. HTML came from https://www.pinterest.com/ (HTTP 200). FAQ

What is the Pinterest favicon.ico URL?

The URL https://www.pinterest.com/favicon.ico did not return an ICO container on 12 November 2026. The files that did download are listed in the table.

What are the Pinterest favicon dimensions?

The pixel sizes below were read from the files, not from a generic size chart. png-144x144-01.png: 144×144 (PNG IHDR) png-16x16-02.png: 16×16 (PNG IHDR) png-48x48-03.png: 48×48 (PNG IHDR) svg-04.svg: 500×500 (SVG viewBox user units, not a pixel bitmap) png-192x192-05.png: 192×192 (PNG IHDR)

What apple-touch-icon does Pinterest publish?

Yes. https://s.pinimg.com/webapp/logo_transparent_144x144-3da7a67b.png saved as png-144x144-01.png.

What brand colour does the Pinterest favicon page record?

#ffffff, from the theme-color meta tag.

Which icon files were saved for Pinterest?

On 12 November 2026 these image files were downloaded for pinterest.com: png-144x144-01.png (png, 3352 bytes, 144×144); png-16x16-02.png (png, 1663 bytes, 16×16); png-48x48-03.png (png, 1618 bytes, 48×48); svg-04.svg (svg, 1151 bytes, 500×500); png-192x192-05.png (png, 11257 bytes, 192×192).

May I use the Pinterest icon on my own site?

No. Logos and favicons belong to their owners and are shown here for reference so you can see the real format and dimensions. For your own site, make a file with the PNG to favicon converter or download a site you control.
